Open source

Vaex, briefly

For several years I co-founded and contributed to Vaex, a Python library for working with tables that are bigger than a computer's memory. I helped shape its machine-learning tools and gave talks about it at Python conferences. Visit vaex.io for the full list of talks and blog posts.

I stepped away from it in 2023. It's an older project now and newer tools do the job better, but it's still there on GitHub. You can find my other open-source work on my GitHub profile.

Background

Before industry: astrophysics

I started out studying how galaxies form. That work taught me statistics, programming, how to work with very large datasets, and how to work on hard problems without knowing whether an answer even exists.

Postdoc, University of Groningen

2014 – 2018

I used data from ESA's Gaia satellite to find stars in the Milky Way that were born elsewhere. As part of the group of Amina Helmi.

PhD, University of Edinburgh

2010 – 2014

I studied how star clusters move around the Andromeda galaxy, to learn how it grew by absorbing smaller galaxies. Under the guidance of Annette Ferguson.
